Privacy

This page describes the product direction for WebLoom’s privacy model. Final launch language will be updated to match the production data flows.

Minimal account data

WebLoom will only request account and billing information needed to provide the service.

Capture data

Project files and capture metadata should be stored only as needed to deliver previews, history and downloads, with clear deletion controls.

Internal systems

Backend providers, repository details, secret keys and internal infrastructure are never intended to be exposed in the public interface.
